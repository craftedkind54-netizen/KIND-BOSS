# Promotion interview question catalog

Eight core categories (24 points total), followed by an unscored Bonus / Troll Check. Select three questions per category. Core questions are based on the supplied SMP rules.pdf; bonus scenarios were supplied by the user.

## General Staff

Basic reports, clear explanations, evidence collection and timely escalation.

### 📖 Staff Knowledge

**Evaluate:** Explain everyday rules and know when to ask higher staff for help.

**Reference:** Staff Rules; Reporting Admin Abuse (pp. 17-19)

1. A player asks you to restore a legally raided base. What do the rules allow you to do?
2. Which uses of spectator are legitimate investigations, and which create a forbidden survival advantage?
3. You receive an abuse report about a moderator. What evidence would you collect and where should it go?
4. A player asks whether an unfamiliar client mod is allowed. What rule criteria would you check before answering?

### ⚔️ Partial-Anarchy Judgment

**Evaluate:** Recognize legal raids, theft and PvP versus protected-area violations.

**Reference:** Anarchy; Spawn Protection; PvP; Scamming; Coordinates (pp. 4-15)

1. A player steals diamonds from an unprotected chest. How would you explain whether this breaks a rule?
2. Someone fires TNT into protected spawn from outside its boundary. Does standing outside make this legal?
3. A raided player shares Minecraft coordinates, then threatens to share a home address. How do these differ?
4. A player loses items in an unprotected portal trap. What would make the trap illegal instead?

### 🔎 Evidence & Investigations

**Evaluate:** Collect clear report details without treating accusations as proof.

**Reference:** Evidence; False Reports; Fake Evidence; Attempts (pp. 19-21)

1. Chat accuses a player of X-ray without proof. What facts would you record and pass to an investigator?
2. A screenshot appears to show a threat. How would you check its context and preserve it privately?
3. A player disconnects once during combat. What would you check before calling it combat logging?
4. Someone reports receiving suspicious items and has stopped using them. What details would you collect?

### ⚖️ Strikes & Punishments

**Evaluate:** Apply routine warnings fairly and recognize serious cases needing escalation.

**Reference:** Punishments (p. 20); rule-specific annotations

1. A player repeatedly spams after a warning. How does the small-offense progression guide your next step?
2. Automod flags part of an ordinary word as cussing. Should the player be punished, and why?
3. A player posts private information. Why might this require urgent escalation rather than an ordinary warning?
4. A player demands a strike for legal PvP outside protection. How would you respond?

### 🛡️ Staff Integrity

**Evaluate:** Keep staff tools out of personal gameplay and report abuse privately.

**Reference:** Staff Rules; No Staff Advantages; Reporting Admin Abuse (pp. 17-19)

1. Your friend asks you to use /give to replace loot lost in a legal raid. What do you say?
2. While investigating, you learn a hidden base location. May you use that knowledge in survival?
3. A moderator asks you to hide evidence of spectator abuse. What private reporting route should you use?
4. You are angry after losing a fight. Why is teleporting away with staff tools unacceptable?

### 🧠 Leadership

**Evaluate:** Stay calm, explain decisions respectfully and accept legitimate criticism.

**Reference:** Discord Rules (pp. 1-4); Reporting Admin Abuse (p. 19); New Situations (p. 25)

1. A player says a server rule is bad. How do you allow criticism while stopping personal harassment?
2. A public argument becomes accusations and dogpiling. How would you redirect it to Support calmly?
3. You cannot determine whether a bug is an exploit. How would you ask for help without claiming certainty?
4. A teammate proposes punishing a legal raid. How would you explain the relevant rule respectfully?

### 🚨 Emergency Judgment

**Evaluate:** Recognize urgent harm, protect private information and call for authorized help.

**Reference:** Private Information (pp. 3-4); Exploits (pp. 8-9); Server Crashing (pp. 16-17); New Situations (p. 25)

1. Private information appears in chat while a player reports a stolen in-game item. What needs immediate attention?
2. You see a suspected crash exploit but lack the access to contain it. What should you report urgently?
3. A player privately reports a new dupe. How would you keep details from spreading and get help?
4. A machine appears to be damaging TPS. What would you observe before calling it an intentional lag machine?

### 🎯 Rank-Specific

**Evaluate:** Handle a basic report responsibly from intake to handoff.

**Reference:** Staff Rules; Evidence; Punishments; New Situations (pp. 17-25)

1. Walk through your first report of protected spawn griefing: facts, evidence, explanation and escalation.
2. How would you explain that a large farm is allowed but may need changes if it harms performance?
3. A new player confuses legal betrayal with account theft. How would you explain the boundary?
4. What would you do if a routine report requires tools or decisions you are not authorized to use?

### 😈 Bonus / Troll Check

**Evaluate:** Show creativity, maturity and calm under playful pressure as general; discuss responsible permissions without acting out the scenario. This bonus is unscored.

**Reference:** User-supplied creative prompts; staff-integrity boundaries from SMP rules.pdf (pp. 17-19)

1. You receive Owner permissions for exactly five minutes. What is the FIRST thing you do? Follow-up: How would you check whether that access was intentional before using it?
2. Without breaking character, convince the board that ketchup is technically a soup. Follow-up: How would you stay friendly if a player disagrees with your soup theory?
3. Defend this statement: noodles belong on pizza. Follow-up: How would you respond calmly to a player who hates this idea?
4. Explain with complete confidence why coffee is secretly a type of tea. Follow-up: How would you admit the difference between a joke and a factual staff answer?
5. A creeper applies for General Staff. Conduct a 20-second interview and decide what questions you would ask it. Follow-up: What basic rule would you ask the creeper to explain?
6. You discover the Owner has secretly replaced every diamond on the server with potatoes. How do you write the incident report? Follow-up: Which facts would you record, and who would you ask for help?
7. Explain why a chicken would make a terrible Senior Staff member without insulting the chicken. Follow-up: How would you give that feedback kindly?
8. You are allowed to create ONE completely useless staff command. What does it do? Follow-up: How would you keep it harmless to players and their items?

## Head of General Staff

Coaching General Staff, checking routine decisions and preparing clear escalations.

### 📖 Staff Knowledge

**Evaluate:** Teach General Staff the rules, staff-tool limits and reporting routes.

**Reference:** Staff Rules; Reporting Admin Abuse (pp. 17-19)

1. How would you train General Staff to distinguish normal client mods from hidden-information cheats?
2. A trainee believes staff are exempt from PvP rules. How would you correct that misunderstanding?
3. How would you teach the private abuse-reporting routes for moderators, Senior Staff and Co-Owners?
4. What should a General Staff handoff contain before a serious cheating report goes to Senior Staff?

### ⚔️ Partial-Anarchy Judgment

**Evaluate:** Coach consistent decisions about legal anarchy and protected areas.

**Reference:** Anarchy; Spawn Protection; PvP; Scamming; Coordinates (pp. 4-15)

1. Two General Staff disagree about restoring an unprotected community build. How would you coach their decision?
2. A trainee treats repeated legal raids as automatic harassment. How would you teach the gameplay/Discord distinction?
3. How would you review a trainee response to a TNT cannon damaging spawn from outside protection?
4. A General Staff member bans all portal traps. What rule examples would you use to correct the advice?

### 🔎 Evidence & Investigations

**Evaluate:** Check routine evidence collection and improve handoffs to Senior Staff.

**Reference:** Evidence; False Reports; Fake Evidence; Attempts (pp. 19-21)

1. A trainee treats several accusations as proof of hacking. How would you improve their evidence handoff?
2. Two routine reports contain conflicting screenshots. What checks would you ask General Staff to perform?
3. A trainee omits the protection boundary from a griefing report. Why does that matter and how do you fix it?
4. How would you teach staff to record possible combat logging without confusing a crash with intent?

### ⚖️ Strikes & Punishments

**Evaluate:** Correct inconsistent warnings and unsupported punishment assumptions.

**Reference:** Punishments (p. 20); rule-specific annotations

1. General Staff give inconsistent responses to repeated spam. How would you align them with the punishment progression?
2. A trainee insists every serious violation needs a warning first. How would you correct this using the rules?
3. A staff member invents a jail duration from a number beside a rule. What must they verify before acting?
4. A trainee punishes an ordinary alt account. How would you review and explain the error?

### 🛡️ Staff Integrity

**Evaluate:** Recognize favoritism or retaliation in the team and route abuse reports fairly.

**Reference:** Staff Rules; No Staff Advantages; Reporting Admin Abuse (pp. 17-19)

1. A General Staff member offers to restore a friend's legally raided base. How would you intervene and document it?
2. A trainee shares investigation-derived coordinates with their survival team. What makes this staff abuse?
3. A moderator you mentor is accused of /give abuse. How do you preserve evidence and route the complaint fairly?
4. A General Staff member wants to punish someone who criticized their decision. How would you address retaliation?

### 🧠 Leadership

**Evaluate:** Mentor General Staff and resolve disagreements calmly using the rules.

**Reference:** Discord Rules (pp. 1-4); Reporting Admin Abuse (p. 19); New Situations (p. 25)

1. How would you coach a General Staff member who turns Support disputes into public arguments?
2. Two trainees disagree about a freecam screenshot. How would you guide them to the hidden-information rule?
3. How would you prepare a concise escalation when routine staff cannot resolve a protection-bypass report?
4. A new mechanic is unclear to your team. How would you prevent staff from presenting guesses as existing rules?

### 🚨 Emergency Judgment

**Evaluate:** Organize first-response tasks and consolidate urgent reports for higher staff.

**Reference:** Private Information (pp. 3-4); Exploits (pp. 8-9); Server Crashing (pp. 16-17); New Situations (p. 25)

1. During a doxxing incident, how would you organize General Staff to limit exposure and notify authorized responders?
2. General Staff are flooded with crash reports. How would you consolidate evidence for Senior Staff?
3. A trainee wants to post dupe instructions publicly as a warning. How would you redirect them?
4. An urgent lag report arrives during routine chat moderation. How would you assign collection and escalation tasks?

### 🎯 Rank-Specific

**Evaluate:** Demonstrate readiness to guide General Staff and document recurring problems.

**Reference:** Staff Rules; Evidence; Punishments; New Situations (pp. 17-25)

1. Design a short coaching exercise that teaches General Staff legal theft versus protected-spawn theft.
2. A General Staff member repeatedly ignores evidence checks. How would you document the pattern for higher review?
3. How would you audit routine reports for favoritism without assuming every mistake is deliberate abuse?
4. What would a clear handoff from your team to Senior Staff look like for suspected ban evasion?

### 😈 Bonus / Troll Check

**Evaluate:** Show creativity, maturity and calm under playful pressure as head general; discuss responsible permissions without acting out the scenario. This bonus is unscored.

**Reference:** User-supplied creative prompts; staff-integrity boundaries from SMP rules.pdf (pp. 17-19)

1. You receive Owner permissions for exactly five minutes. What is the FIRST thing you do? Follow-up: What would you teach General Staff about unexpected permissions?
2. Without breaking character, convince the board that ketchup is technically a soup. Follow-up: How would you coach a trainee whose soup debate becomes personal?
3. Defend this statement: noodles belong on pizza. Follow-up: How would you settle two trainees arguing about your pizza claim?
4. Explain with complete confidence why coffee is secretly a type of tea. Follow-up: How would you stop a trainee from presenting your joke as official guidance?
5. A creeper applies for General Staff. Conduct a 20-second interview and decide what questions you would ask it. Follow-up: What feedback would you give a trainee who interviewed the creeper?
6. You discover the Owner has secretly replaced every diamond on the server with potatoes. How do you write the incident report? Follow-up: How would you help General Staff prepare a clear handoff about the potatoes?
7. Explain why a chicken would make a terrible Senior Staff member without insulting the chicken. Follow-up: How would you model constructive feedback for a trainee watching?
8. You are allowed to create ONE completely useless staff command. What does it do? Follow-up: How would you teach the team when using it would be inappropriate?

## Senior Staff

Serious investigations, disputed punishments, evidence review and support for General Staff.

### 📖 Staff Knowledge

**Evaluate:** Lead serious case reviews and explain escalation routes to General Staff.

**Reference:** Staff Rules; Reporting Admin Abuse (pp. 17-19)

1. As Senior Staff, how would you take over a General Staff cheating report while keeping evidence and conclusions separate?
2. A moderator is accused of spectator abuse. How would you investigate, and where must a complaint about Senior Staff go?
3. How would you distinguish permitted investigative use of inventory logs from using those logs for survival advantage?
4. How would you explain to General Staff when serious violations may skip the ordinary warning progression?

### ⚔️ Partial-Anarchy Judgment

**Evaluate:** Resolve complex cases involving legal raids, hidden-information cheats and harassment.

**Reference:** Anarchy; Spawn Protection; PvP; Scamming; Coordinates (pp. 4-15)

1. A legal raid is followed by sustained personal attacks on Discord. How would you separate the two parts of the case?
2. A base was found using either Nether trails or ESP. How would the method change your ruling?
3. A player claims a freecam session was only for screenshots, but hidden stashes were found. What matters to your decision?
4. A trap outside spawn injures players still inside protection. How would you investigate the bypass claim?

### 🔎 Evidence & Investigations

**Evaluate:** Corroborate cheating, ban evasion and exploit evidence; review conflicting accounts.

**Reference:** Evidence; False Reports; Fake Evidence; Attempts (pp. 19-21)

1. General Staff suspect ban evasion on a borrowed account. How would you corroborate evasion without treating every alt as guilty?
2. Anti-cheat alerts and video disagree about a combat incident. How would you weigh context and alternative explanations?
3. Several players received duped items. How would you distinguish knowing storage or distribution from prompt reporting?
4. An appeal alleges that screenshots were fabricated. What original records would you compare before deciding?

### ⚖️ Strikes & Punishments

**Evaluate:** Review serious punishments and appeals for evidence, intent and proportionality.

**Reference:** Punishments (p. 20); rule-specific annotations

1. A cheating case is serious but disputed. How would you justify action using evidence and severity rather than accusation volume?
2. How would you review a punishment for combat logging when a combat-tag system may not have been active?
3. A player tried but failed to crash the server. What evidence of intent is needed before treating the attempt as a violation?
4. An appeal shows a punishment was imposed for legal unprotected griefing. How would you address the decision and explain it?

### 🛡️ Staff Integrity

**Evaluate:** Investigate moderator abuse fairly and escalate Senior Staff complaints to leadership.

**Reference:** Staff Rules; No Staff Advantages; Reporting Admin Abuse (pp. 17-19)

1. A moderator used spectator to locate a base and later raided it. How would you investigate the link without favoritism?
2. A Senior Staff colleague is accused of hiding duped items. How would you preserve the report and escalate to Co-Owner or Owner?
3. Your survival ally is involved in an exploit case you are reviewing. How would you manage that conflict of interest?
4. You discover your own investigation exposed base information to a teammate. How would you report and address the mistake?

### 🧠 Leadership

**Evaluate:** Support General Staff, explain disputed outcomes and remain professional under pressure.

**Reference:** Discord Rules (pp. 1-4); Reporting Admin Abuse (p. 19); New Situations (p. 25)

1. General Staff disagree about an exploit case. How would you lead an evidence-based review and explain the outcome?
2. A player criticizes your appeal decision publicly. How would you preserve their right to criticize while stopping harassment?
3. How would you coach General Staff after they confused a permitted automatic farm with forbidden software automation?
4. A new bug causes harm but no rule addresses it clearly. How would you escalate uncertainty without applying a future rule retroactively?

### 🚨 Emergency Judgment

**Evaluate:** Coordinate authorized containment of exploits while preserving evidence and privacy.

**Reference:** Private Information (pp. 3-4); Exploits (pp. 8-9); Server Crashing (pp. 16-17); New Situations (p. 25)

1. An active dupe is spreading while chat fills with accusations. How would you coordinate containment, evidence and communication?
2. A crash exploit is suspected in one area. What authorized containment and evidence checks would you prioritize?
3. A farm causes severe lag, but intent is unclear. How would you reduce harm without assuming it is a deliberate lag machine?
4. Doxxing and cheating reports arrive together. How would you protect private information while delegating the investigation?

### 🎯 Rank-Specific

**Evaluate:** Carry serious investigations from a General Staff handoff to a reasoned outcome.

**Reference:** Staff Rules; Evidence; Punishments; New Situations (pp. 17-25)

1. Walk through taking a General Staff report of ban evasion from initial evidence to a reasoned review outcome.
2. How would you review a disputed major punishment when the reporter and accused provide conflicting logs?
3. A group used a protection exploit, but members played different roles. How would you assess each person's knowledge and conduct?
4. What should your escalation to leadership include when a serious case implicates another Senior Staff member?

**Evaluate:** Show creativity, maturity and calm under playful pressure as senior; discuss responsible permissions without acting out the scenario. This bonus is unscored.

**Reference:** User-supplied creative prompts; staff-integrity boundaries from SMP rules.pdf (pp. 17-19)

## Owner

Final accountability, fair policy decisions and independent review of complaints about the Owner.

### 📖 Staff Knowledge

**Evaluate:** Accept that staff rules apply to the Owner and enable review by other highest-ranking staff.
